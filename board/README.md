(Articles of Incorporation, § 6); all corporate powers are exercised by or under the authority

## Composition rules

- **Articles of Incorporation § 7(b):** not fewer than 1 and not more than 15 directors, the exact number fixed by the Bylaws.
- **Bylaws § 2.1:** not fewer than 1 and not more than 7 directors. Directors must be at least 18, have demonstrated interest in the Corporation's objectives, and be able to decide and address issues impacting the Corporation.
- **Terms:** one year, or until a successor is elected and qualified, unless earlier removed.
- **Officers (Bylaws § 10.1):** a President and a Secretary are required; Treasurer, Vice Presidents and others are optional. One person may hold more than one office.

## Meetings

The Board must meet at least once per year (Bylaws § 3.1). Meetings may be held by any means
where all participating directors can simultaneously hear and communicate with each other.
Quorum is a majority of directors (§ 3.5). The Board may also act by unanimous written consent
without a meeting (§ 3.4).

## Minutes

Bylaws § 16.1 requires the Corporation to keep permanent minutes of all Board meetings and all
actions taken without a meeting. Minutes belong in `board/minutes/` as `YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
